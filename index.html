How I work

Start with the job. Respect the terrain.

  1. 01

    Get close to the work

    Understand the environment, handoffs, workarounds, policies, and failure points before drawing the happy path.

  2. 02

    Reduce the burden

    Look for what the system can remember, explain, prioritize, or automate so people can focus on judgment and service.

  3. 03

    Prototype the whole flow

    Design beyond a single screen. Validate states, transitions, edge cases, and the physical context around the interface.

  4. 04

    Stay through delivery

    Work closely with product and engineering until the experience survives real constraints and lands with integrity.

About

Designer, potter, bird watcher.

I’m Matt Moore, a Senior UX Designer leading the retail experience team at Bass Pro Shops. Since joining in 2023, I’ve worked across customer service, store operations, inventory, appraisal, and compliance tools.

Before that, I designed products at Garmin on the Forerunner team. I earned a BFA in User Experience Design from Savannah College of Art & Design, graduating magna cum laude, and my earlier work received Red Dot and Indigo Design recognition.

I work remotely from Overland Park, Kansas. Away from the screen, I’m usually with my family, throwing pottery, or trying to get a better photograph of whatever bird just landed in the yard.
